Joby Aviation (NYSE:JOBY) has begun maiden flights of its FAA conforming aircraft as it prepares for commercial air taxi services. The company is progressing under the FAA's eVTOL Integration Pilot Program, marking a shift from development toward operational readiness. Partnerships with Uber and Delta Airlines are part of Joby's plan to build out future air taxi networks and customer access. Joby Aviation, listed as NYSE:JOBY, focuses on electric vertical takeoff and landing aircraft aimed at short haul urban and regional trips. The latest flights with FAA conforming aircraft come as the broader urban air mobility sector works through certification, infrastructure planning, and customer adoption questions. For investors, these operational and regulatory steps highlight more of the practical path from prototype to potential commercial service. As Joby works with partners like Uber and Delta and engages with the FAA, the key issues to monitor include certification progress, safety outcomes, and how early routes and pricing structures are defined.

Pfizer and its partners have granted Rigel Pharmaceuticals an exclusive license to commercialize VEPPANU (vepdegestrant), the first FDA approved oral PROTAC for advanced breast cancer. The agreement follows Phase 3 data that support clinical benefit in a difficult to treat patient group and inclusion of VEPPANU in NCCN breast cancer guidelines. The deal introduces a new commercialization pathway for VEPPANU and adds another partnership driven revenue channel for Pfizer. For investors watching NYSE:PFE, this move comes with the stock trading at $25.79 and a mixed return profile. The share price is up 2.4% year to date and 19.8% over the past year, while the 3 year and 5 year returns show declines of 19.5% and 14.4% respectively. The agreement adds a fresh product story to a company that has already seen several shifts in its portfolio in recent years. VEPPANU sits at the intersection of oncology and targeted protein degradation, an area many investors track closely for potential long term therapy platforms. While outcomes are uncertain, this type of licensing deal can help clarify how Pfizer might use partnerships alongside in house development to build out future revenue streams and clinical presence in cancer care.

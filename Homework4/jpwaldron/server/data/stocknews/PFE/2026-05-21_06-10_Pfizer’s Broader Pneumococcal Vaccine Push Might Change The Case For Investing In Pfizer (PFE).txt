To own Pfizer today, you need to believe that its late stage pipeline in vaccines, oncology and rare diseases can gradually replace revenue pressure from patent expirations and drug pricing reforms. The positive Phase 2 data for the 25 valent infant pneumococcal vaccine fits that pipeline story, but it does not change the near term focus on execution risk around the coming patent cliff and pricing headwinds, which remain the key swing factors for the stock. Among recent announcements, the expanded European approval for HYMPAVZI (marstacimab) in hemophilia with inhibitors is especially relevant. It shows Pfizer adding differentiated, higher value treatments in complex rare disease niches, alongside its broader vaccine work. For investors watching how new launches and label expansions might offset loss of exclusivity on drugs like Ibrance and XTANDI, HYMPAVZI’s progress is another proof point in that pipeline driven transition story. Yet despite this pipeline progress, investors should not overlook the growing impact of government price controls and reimbursement reforms on Pfizer’s future economics...

Multiple firms, including Wells Fargo, Citi, Oppenheimer, Truist, Barclays, JPMorgan, Evercore ISI, Argus, DA Davidson and others, have recently lifted their Caterpillar price targets, signaling a reset in how they frame the stock’s valuation relative to earnings and growth optionality. Wells Fargo cites an updated view on private non residential construction and data center related activity. It uses this to support a higher target for Caterpillar as exposure to these end markets is reflected in its models. Citi points to indications from the ConExpo construction trade show, highlighting a pipeline of large projects and a constructive view on U.S. construction and rental machinery. This feeds into its Caterpillar assumptions. Oppenheimer references discussions with Caterpillar’s Chief Digital Officer around the company’s role in physical AI. It factors this into its long term earnings framework and a higher target level. Despite the cluster of target increases and a Morgan Stanley upgrade, several reports still frame investor debate around how sustainable any multiple re rating might be, especially once cycle benefits fade or if construction and industrial demand softens. ProPetro’s PROPWR unit and Nscale each entered multi year power agreements with Caterpillar, covering up to 4.1 gigawatts of generation capacity for data centers, oil and gas, industrial customers and the Monarch AI campus in West Virginia.

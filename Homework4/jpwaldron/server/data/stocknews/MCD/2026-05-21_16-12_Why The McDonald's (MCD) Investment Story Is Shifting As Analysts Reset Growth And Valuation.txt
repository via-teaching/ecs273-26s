McDonald's latest analyst update trims its fair value price target from US$345.00 to US$330.00, signaling a slightly more cautious stance on what the stock might be worth. This change lines up with recent Street research, where bullish and bearish views are both active as firms reset expectations and adjust their targets. Jefferies recently added McDonald's to its Franchise Picks list, which it describes as containing the firm's highest conviction Buy rated stocks, signaling confidence in the brand and its execution. Tigress Financial lifted its McDonald's price target to US$385 from US$360, pointing to what it describes as a powerful global brand, rapid unit expansion, AI driven efficiencies, and an asset light franchise model. KeyBanc raised its target to US$354 from US$340 and highlighted confidence in the current momentum of the U.S. business based on industry conversations and card data. CFRA upgraded McDonald's, indicating a more positive stance even as other firms reassess their numbers. Multiple firms including JPMorgan, RBC Capital, Morgan Stanley, Deutsche Bank, Baird, KeyBanc, Barclays and TD Cowen recently cut McDonald's price targets, suggesting a more cautious view on valuation and near term growth assumptions. Erste Group downgraded McDonald's, which points to concerns around execution or upside potential at recent trading levels, even though specific reasons were not detailed in the available summaries. McDonald's plans to roll out a lower priced value menu in April, featuring items priced at US$3 and below and new US$4 breakfast meal deals aimed at budget conscious customers. The company is preparing new bundled deals that group low priced menu items across different dayparts, which could influence how customers choose between value and premium options. McDonald's is set to add energy drinks to its menu, expanding its beverage range and potentially affecting traffic patterns around snack and afternoon visits. McDonald's and the Chicago Fire Football Club agreed to a naming rights deal for the club's new US$750m stadium, McDonald's Park, which is expected to feature a flagship restaurant, fan experiences and community programming when it opens in 2028.

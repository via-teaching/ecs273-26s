Title: Blockchain.com Files Confidentially for IPO as Crypto Listings Regain Momentum
Date: 2026-05-21 13:45
URL: https://www.cryptoprowl.com/releases/blockchaincom-files-confidentially-for-ipo-as-crypto-listings-regain-momentum-5571?.tsrc=rss
Content: Blockchain.com has taken a formal step toward the public markets after years of waiting for the right listing window. The crypto brokerage and wallet provider has confidentially filed for an initial public offering, Bloomberg reported Thursday, citing people familiar with the matter. The filing does not guarantee a listing, but it gives Blockchain.com a path to move through the regulatory review process while keeping detailed financial disclosures private until closer to a potential roadshow. Crypto’s listing pipeline has started to look more active again as larger digital asset firms test whether public-market appetite has recovered. Kraken, Gemini (NASDAQ:$GEMI), Bullish (NYSE:$BLSH) and BitGo (NYSE:$BTGO) have all been linked to IPO plans or confidential filings, giving investors a broader set of exchange, custody and infrastructure names to watch as the sector moves closer to traditional finance. For Blockchain.com, the filing follows a long reset from the last cycle. The company was valued at about $14 billion in a 2022 funding round led by Lightspeed Venture Partners, with major participation from Baillie Gifford. A later $110 million Series E financing in 2023 reportedly cut that valuation to less than half, reflecting the pressure that hit crypto platforms after the Terra, Three Arrows Capital and FTX collapses. The company has been preparing more visibly for a listing. Last year, Blockchain.com hired Justin Evans, the former head of crypto investment banking at Goldman Sachs (NYSE:$GS), as chief financial officer, and added Mike Wilcox, a former Point72 portfolio manager and Velocity Global CFO, as chief operating officer. It later promoted president Lane Kasselman to co-CEO alongside founder Peter Smith as part of its public-market push. A listing would test how much appetite remains for crypto platforms that survived the downturn and rebuilt around brokerage, wallet and institutional services.

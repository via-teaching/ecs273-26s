On March 31, 2026, NVIDIA Corporation and Marvell Technology announced that NVIDIA would invest US$2.00 billion via convertible preferred stock and enter a broad partnership linking Marvell’s custom XPUs, networking and optical solutions into NVIDIA’s NVLink Fusion AI infrastructure and AI‑RAN ecosystem.

Just days later, Marvell added nearly US$1.00 billion of new 5.300% senior unsecured notes due 2036, underscoring how the NVIDIA partnership is being paired with substantial balance sheet financing to support AI data center and telecom build‑outs.

To own Marvell today, you need to believe its heavy tilt toward AI data centers and custom silicon can translate into durable revenue and cash generation, despite customer and project concentration. The NVIDIA partnership and US$2.00 billion preferred investment sharpen that story and appear to support the near term AI data center buildout catalyst, while also amplifying the key risk that Marvell’s fortunes are increasingly tied to a small club of hyperscale partners.

The most relevant recent move alongside NVIDIA’s investment is Marvell’s US$998.85 million issuance of 5.300% senior unsecured notes due 2036. Layering this debt on top of NVIDIA’s capital suggests Marvell is committing meaningful resources to AI infrastructure and telecom projects. That can accelerate its custom XPU and optics roadmap, but it also raises the stakes if large design wins slip, internal customer chips take priority, or data center spending slows.

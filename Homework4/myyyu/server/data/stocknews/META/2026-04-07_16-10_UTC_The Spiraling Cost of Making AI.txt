Title: The Spiraling Cost of Making AI
Date: 2026-04-07 16:10 UTC
URL: https://finance.yahoo.com/m/fb9a9db9-96a6-3fe9-9921-c3ce4e4383c7/the-spiraling-cost-of-making.html?.tsrc=rss
Content:
Spending money to make money is taking on a whole new meaning in the artificial-intelligence world. The public is already familiar with the exorbitant capital spending of the world’s largest tech companies as they race to build up their AI capabilities. OpenAI and Anthropic expect to spend nearly $65 billion combined this year just on the costs to train and operate their AI models, according to financial documents obtained by The Wall Street Journal.

Title: Delta Air hikes checked baggage fees as jet fuel prices soar
Date: 2026-04-07 18:08 UTC
URL: https://finance.yahoo.com/video/delta-air-hikes-checked-baggage-180823122.html?.tsrc=rss
Content:
<span>STORY: Delta on Tuesday said it will raise fees for checked bags on domestic and select international flights as the airline seeks to offset soaring fuel costs linked to the war in the Middle East.</span><span>It's the first hike in Delta's domestic baggage fees in two years and follows similar moves by rivals United Airlines and JetBlue.</span><span>Airlines worldwide are grappling with sharply higher fuel prices after tensions in the Middle East disrupted the delivery of oil through the Strait of Hormuz.</span><span>That's driven up airlines' operating costs and squeezed margins.</span><span>:: $85.00- $90.00</span><span>Jet fuel had averaged about $85 to $90 a barrel before the U.S. and Israel launched their first strikes on Iran in February.</span><span>:: $150.00- $200.00</span><span>But jet fuel has surged to around $150 to $200 a barrel in recent weeks.</span><span>Delta said fees for first and second checked bags on domestic and select short-haul international flights will increase by $10 each, while the cost of a third checked bag will rise by $50.</span><span>As a result, the fee for a first checked bag will increase to $45, the second to $55 and the third to $200.</span><span>The new prices go into effect on Wednesday.</span>

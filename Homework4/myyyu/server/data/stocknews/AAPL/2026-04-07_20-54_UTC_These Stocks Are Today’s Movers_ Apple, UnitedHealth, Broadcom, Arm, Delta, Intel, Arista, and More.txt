Stocks traded sideways Tuesday after President Donald Trump declared that “a whole civilization will die tonight” ahead of his 8 p.m. ET deadline for Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. The federal government on Monday pledged to pay insurers in the Medicare program an average increase of 2.48% next year, well above an originally proposed rate of just 0.09% in January.

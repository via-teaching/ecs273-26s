Apple ( NASDAQ:AAPL ) is hitting a few bumps with its foldable iPhone, and it looks like the timeline could slip.

The issues are coming up during the engineering test phase, where things are proving more complicated than expected. Fixing those problems is taking longer, and that could push production and shipments back by a few months. It is still early in the process, but the delays suggest the device is not quite ready to move forward yet.

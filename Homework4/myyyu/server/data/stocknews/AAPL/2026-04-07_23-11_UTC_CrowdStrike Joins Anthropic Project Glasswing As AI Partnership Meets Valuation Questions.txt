CrowdStrike Holdings (NasdaqGS:CRWD) has been selected as a partner in Anthropic's "Project Glasswing".

The company gains early access to Anthropic’s Claude Mythos Preview AI model for defensive cybersecurity work.

This partnership places CrowdStrike alongside large technology players such as Amazon, Microsoft, and Apple within the project.

CrowdStrike focuses on cloud delivered cybersecurity, with a core platform built around endpoint protection, threat intelligence, and security operations. Anthropic’s selection of CrowdStrike for Project Glasswing connects one of the larger independent cybersecurity platforms with an emerging generation of AI models aimed at security use cases. For investors watching NasdaqGS:CRWD, this link between AI research and security products is an important part of how the company is positioning its technology stack.

The Anthropic partnership indicates that future security workflows could lean more heavily on AI assistance, both for detection and response. For CrowdStrike, access to Claude Mythos Preview may influence how quickly it can test new AI driven features, differentiate its products, and deepen its role in broader industry collaboration with large technology companies.

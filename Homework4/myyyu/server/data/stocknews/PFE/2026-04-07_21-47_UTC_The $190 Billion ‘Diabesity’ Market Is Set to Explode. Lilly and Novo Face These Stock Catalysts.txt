Title: The $190 Billion ‘Diabesity’ Market Is Set to Explode. Lilly and Novo Face These Stock Catalysts.

Content:
The recent fixation on weight-loss drugs, a catchall term referring to GLP-1 medications like Wegovy and Zepbound, has brought Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk squarely into the limelight. The combined market for both indications—chronic weight management and diabetes—is projected to be enormous. Morgan Stanley forecasts a “diabesity” total addressable market in the neighborhood of $190 billion in 2035.

Title: Analysts Are Slow to Recognize a Crisis

Content:
Market efficiency is one of the most important ideas in finance, but investors can take it too literally. It’s possible the stock market’s mild reaction to the war in Iran—the S&P 500 is down by just 3.9%—reflects everything smart people know about the conflict’s economic effects. A big reason is psychological: It takes lots of evidence to convince people something unthinkable might be about to happen like major banks collapsing, a pandemic or actual fuel shortages.

Royal Caribbean Group and Bank of America have launched the Royal ONE and Royal ONE Plus Visa Signature tri-branded credit cards, allowing guests to earn and redeem rewards across Royal Caribbean, Celebrity Cruises, and Silversea with tiered point multipliers, travel perks, and no foreign transaction fees.

This tri-branded program deepens Royal Caribbean’s loyalty ecosystem by tying everyday card spending directly to cruise savings and onboard experiences across its three core brands.

To own Royal Caribbean today, you need to believe that experience-focused travel and the company’s expanding fleet and destinations can support healthy earnings while it manages fuel, debt, and macro uncertainty. In the near term, booking trends and pricing remain the key catalyst, while volatile oil prices are a major risk. The new Royal ONE tri-branded cards strengthen the loyalty ecosystem, but they are unlikely to materially shift those near term drivers by themselves.

The most relevant recent update alongside the card launch is Royal Caribbean’s solid 2025 financial performance, with US$17,935,000,000 in revenue and US$4,268,000,000 in net income. That backdrop of strong profitability and an improving balance sheet gives more context to the Royal ONE program, which plugs into an existing loyalty and cash generation story rather than trying to fix a weak core business.

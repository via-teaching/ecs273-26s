JPMorgan lifting its price target on InnovAge Holding to US$7 from US$5 signals that its refreshed model supports a higher valuation anchor for the shares.

The updated model from JPMorgan gives investors a more current reference point for what one large research house thinks the business could be worth, which some readers may view as a constructive data point when comparing to the current share price.

Despite the higher target, JPMorgan keeps an Underweight rating on InnovAge, which indicates that the firm still sees better opportunities elsewhere on a risk and reward basis.

The fact that JPMorgan’s stance did not change along with the target suggests that the revision is more about model mechanics rather than a shift in conviction around execution or growth prospects.

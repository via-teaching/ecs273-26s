Title: Goldman Sachs Upgrades Netflix to Buy Before Earnings
Date: 2026-04-06 18:56 UTC
URL: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/goldman-sachs-upgrades-netflix-buy-185655800.html?.tsrc=rss
Content:
This article first appeared on GuruFocus .

Netflix ( NASDAQ:NFLX ) rose 1.56% intraday, after Goldman Sachs ( NYSE:GS ) upgraded the stock to Buy from Neutral, citing improved risk-reward ahead of its April 16 earnings report. The firm also raised its price target to $120, implying roughly 26% upside from current levels.

The upgrade follows a 16% decline in Netflix shares over the past six months, a drop Goldman linked in part to overhang from the company's now-abandoned bid for Warner Bros. Discovery's ( NASDAQ:WBD ) assets. With Netflix having walked away from that deal and collecting a roughly $2.8 billion termination fee from Paramount Skydance ( NASDAQ:PSKY ), analyst Sheridan sees the company returning to a standalone execution story with room for a positive estimates revision cycle.

Goldman's bull case runs on three tracks. The first is revenue from the March 2026 U.S. price hikes, which it estimates could add a cumulative $3 billion across 2026 and 2027 combined. The second is GAAP operating margin expansion of around 250 basis points annually over the next three years. Finally, capital returns, with management's $11 billion free cash flow target for 2026 described as potentially conservative now that M&A is off the table.

Advertising monetization is the longer-horizon driver. Goldman projects Netflix's ad revenues will grow from approximately $1.5 billion in 2025 to around $4.5 billion by 2027 and close to $9.5 billion by 2030.

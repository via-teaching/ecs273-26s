LOS ANGELES, CA - April 24, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Onshore basins of genuine scale that remain undrilled are increasingly rare. Most of the world's major hydrocarbon-producing regions have been systematically tested over the past half-century, leaving frontier opportunities concentrated in geographies with challenging logistics, complex permitting, or historically limiting macroeconomic conditions. Greenland Energy holds rights to up to 70% working interest across three onshore licenses covering more than 2 million acres in East Greenland's Jameson Land Basin. The company has contracted Stampede Drilling for Arctic-rated rig services alongside agreements with Halliburton, Desgagns, and IPT Well Solutions to support its 2026 drilling campaign. Where such basins remain, they carry a combination of technical risk and optionality that draws a specific type of investor interest.

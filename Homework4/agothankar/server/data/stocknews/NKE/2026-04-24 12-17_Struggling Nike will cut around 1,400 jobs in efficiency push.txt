Title: Struggling Nike will cut around 1,400 jobs in efficiency push
Date: 2026-04-24 12:17
URL: https://finance.yahoo.com/video/struggling-nike-cut-around-1-121726200.html?.tsrc=rss

<span>STORY: Nike said on Thursday it is laying off about 1,400 people to streamline workflows.</span><span>As the struggling sportswear company fights through a years-long sales slump.</span><span>In a memo to employees on Thursday, the company said it would cut jobs in global operations, mainly technology.</span><span>It accounts for a little less than 2% of the global workforce.</span><span>Shares edged up about 0.5% in after-hours trading but shares have lost more than half their value in the last three years.</span><span>:: On</span><span>Nimbler competitors like On, Hoka and Anta have won more shelf space.</span><span>It is the latest in a string of job cuts.</span><span>Most recently 775 roles were axed in January in a bid to speed up automation.</span><span>:: Nike</span><span>CEO Elliott Hill, who took the helm in 2024 has vowed to re-center the brand.</span><span>Focusing on core sports like running and soccer, and bringing new and innovative shoes to market quickly.</span><span>However, efforts to wow the market with new, must-have sneakers have been inconsistent.</span><span>Analysts believe Nike should be further along in its recovery and the job losses are unsurprising.</span><span>According to the memo, the cuts will allow Nike to better integrate its supply chains and to focus its technology operations in two main hubs in Oregon and India.</span>

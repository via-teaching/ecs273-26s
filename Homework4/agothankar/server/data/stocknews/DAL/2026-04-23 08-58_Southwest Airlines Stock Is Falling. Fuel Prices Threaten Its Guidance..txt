Title: Southwest Airlines Stock Is Falling. Fuel Prices Threaten Its Guidance.
Date: 2026-04-23 08:58
URL: https://finance.yahoo.com/m/a9c36198-8602-3e02-82c9-8f90a77db4ee/southwest-airlines-stock-is.html?.tsrc=rss

Southwest Airlines slightly missed Wall Street’s expectations for the first quarter and declined to update its full-year guidance as the air carrier, along with the entire industry, grapples with increased fuel costs. Wall Street had expected a first-quarter profit of 47 cents a share with sales totaling $7.27 billion, according to FactSet. The carrier said first-quarter fuel costs were $2.73 a gallon, above its prior guidance of about $2.40, which increased fuel expenses by $164 million and represented approximately a 22 cents a share headwind.

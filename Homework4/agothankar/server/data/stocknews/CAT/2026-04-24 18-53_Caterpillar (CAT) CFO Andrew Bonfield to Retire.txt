Caterpillar Inc. (NYSE:CAT) is one of the 10 Best Performing Dow Stocks So Far in 2026. On April 9, 2026, Reuters reported that Caterpillar Inc. (NYSE:CAT) announced that CFO Andrew Bonfield will retire in October. The firm has chosen company veteran Kyle Epley as his successor. Epley will take over the post in May, having previously served as senior vice president of Global Finance Services. Bonfield joined in 2018 and managed financial strategy during a period of record 2025 revenue and mounting tariff pressures. The firm warned of $2.6 billion in tariff-related expenditures in 2026. Bonfield will serve as senior advisor until October 1 to assist with the transition. On April 8, 2026, Caterpillar Inc. (NYSE:CAT)’s board retained its quarterly dividend at $1.51 per share. The dividend will be paid on May 19, 2026, to shareholders of record on April 20. The corporation stated that it has paid dividends annually from inception and quarterly since 1933.

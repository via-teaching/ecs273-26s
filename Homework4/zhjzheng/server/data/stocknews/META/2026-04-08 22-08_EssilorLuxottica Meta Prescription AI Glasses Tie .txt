EssilorLuxottica Société anonyme and Meta have expanded their partnership with new Ray-Ban Meta optical-first smart glasses tailored for prescription wearers.
The updated product line introduces advanced AI features such as live translation and nutrition tracking, as well as broader integration with music and fitness platforms.
The glasses are being rolled out across major global markets, targeting users who prioritize vision correction alongside connected eyewear features.
For EssilorLuxottica (ENXTPA:EL), the focus on prescription-first AI glasses directly connects its core optical business with wearable technology. The shares recently traded around €200.7, with a 3.5% gain over the past week and returns of 23.9% over 3 years and 56.9% over 5 years. This new product family extends the company’s reach beyond traditional frames and lenses into more feature rich connected eyewear.
The expanded Meta collaboration broadens EssilorLuxottica’s potential user base, especially for customers who need prescription lenses but also want AI features in everyday wear. Investors tracking ENXTPA:EL may view this as an effort to build a larger role at the intersection of vision care, consumer technology and lifestyle accessories, supported by the company’s existing global distribution network.

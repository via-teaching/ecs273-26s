Global investors are growing cautious on stocks, and the hit the world economy is likely to take from the U.S. war with Iran, but not to the point where they’re ready to bet against the market, which is near all-time highs heading into the start of second quarter. Bank of America’s benchmark Fund Manager Survey, published monthly, suggests the world’s biggest investors are expecting the fastest inflation pressures in five years, the slowest GDP growth in four and elevated oil prices for the remainder of 2026.

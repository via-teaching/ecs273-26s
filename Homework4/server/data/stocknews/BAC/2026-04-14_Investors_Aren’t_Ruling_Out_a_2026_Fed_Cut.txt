Investors are no longer seeing a Fed cut this year as a long shot. To be sure, the consensus is that the Federal Reserve is much likelier to hold rates steady as officials grapple with an energy shock induced by the war in Iran that has already caused inflation to jump. Global investor sentiment reached its most bearish level since last June.

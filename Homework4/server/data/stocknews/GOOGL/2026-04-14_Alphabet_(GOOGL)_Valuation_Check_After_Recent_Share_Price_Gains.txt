Despite its dominance and innovation, Alphabet is currently the cheapest stock among the “Magnificent 7” (the seven largest U.S. tech companies by market capitalization). In Q3 2024, Alphabet reported $76.4 billion in revenue, with advertising contributing approximately 78% of total income.

Meta Platforms (META, Financials) is heading into a New Mexico trial that could force major changes to Facebook and Instagram for younger users.

The state claims Meta designed its platforms to keep young users engaged and failed to protect children from harm. New Mexico is asking the court to require changes such as age verification, limits on autoplay and infinite scroll, and algorithm changes for minors.
The trial follows a March jury finding that Meta misrepresented the safety of Facebook and Instagram for young users. That verdict included $375 million in damages.
Meta disputes the claims, saying it has taken steps to protect teens and that there is no clear scientific evidence linking its platforms to youth mental health problems.
For investors, the case adds to legal and regulatory pressure already facing Meta in the U.S. and Europe. The next focus will be whether the judge orders platform changes that could affect user engagement or costs.

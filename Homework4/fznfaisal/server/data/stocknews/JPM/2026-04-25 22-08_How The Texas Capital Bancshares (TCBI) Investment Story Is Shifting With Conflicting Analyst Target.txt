Several firms, including JPMorgan, Piper Sandler and RBC Capital, recently raised their price targets on Texas Capital Bancshares by amounts ranging from $4 to $5, signaling increased confidence in the current setup and execution path.
Earlier in the year, Keefe Bruyette, TD Cowen and Citi each moved targets higher by $7 to $13, which points to a group of analysts that sees room for valuation to align more closely with their updated models.
The cluster of upward revisions in late January and again in April suggests that, for some firms, recent company actions and positioning are lining up with their expectations for future growth opportunities.
BofA reduced its price target by $2, and JPMorgan cut its target by $5 on April 1, indicating that not all firms are comfortable with the current risk reward profile or the pace of progress.
The presence of both increases and cuts across the same period leaves a split view, which is useful if you are weighing whether current valuation already prices in much of the execution and growth story.

Lululemon Athletica has appointed Heidi O’Neill as its next CEO.
O’Neill is scheduled to take on the role in September 2026.
She brings extensive leadership experience from senior roles at Nike.
Lululemon Athletica, listed on NasdaqGS:LULU, is making this leadership change after a challenging share price period. The stock last closed at $141.66, with returns of a 13% decline over the past 7 days, a 12% decline over 30 days, and a 32.8% decline year to date. Over 1, 3, and 5 years, returns of 47.7%, 62.5%, and 58.7% decline highlight a tough run for long-term holders.
For investors, the upcoming CEO transition is an important development to watch, especially given Heidi O’Neill’s background in global expansion, product work, and digital growth. As the handover date in September 2026 approaches, attention is likely to focus on how Lululemon’s leadership plans to position the brand, allocate capital, and respond to competitive pressures in activewear and athleisure.

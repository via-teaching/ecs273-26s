Price targets on Coca-Cola have clustered in a tight band, with recent calls at US$87, US$88 and US$90, while model fair value sits at US$83.67. This gives you a clear sense of where expectations are being set. Research firms are pairing these targets with a renewed Top Pick label in North American consumer staples and upbeat feedback from recent conferences, which shapes how risk and reward are being framed for the stock. Morgan Stanley has named Coca-Cola its Top Pick in beverages and its Top Pick overall in North American consumer staples, signaling confidence in how the company is positioned within the sector.
Jefferies, BofA, Citi, JPMorgan, Evercore ISI and Morgan Stanley have all raised price targets on Coca-Cola, indicating that several firms see support for valuations above prior assumptions.
Morgan Stanley highlights beverages as an area where it sees stronger pricing power and meaningful contribution from product innovation, which it links to Coca-Cola’s appeal in the current consumer setup.
BofA flags that Coca-Cola “sounded positive” at the CAGNY conference and describes the update as “bottler friendly,” which supports the view that execution with key partners remains an area of focus.
Morgan Stanley’s prior “timing driven stop out” shows that even bullish firms see timing risk around entry points, so some analysts may still view the risk or reward profile as sensitive to shorter term swings.

Target plans to require all cereals it sells to be made without certified synthetic colors by the end of May. This change affects large packaged food and beverage suppliers, including Coca-Cola, and may shape consumer attention on ingredients across grocery categories.
Coca-Cola plans to discontinue its frozen products in the U.S. and Canada, signaling a reshaping of its product portfolio in those markets.
The Board approved Coca-Cola's 64th consecutive annual dividend increase, setting the quarterly dividend at US$0.53 per share. This is equivalent to US$2.12 per share on an annual basis in 2025, with the first quarter dividend payable on April 1, 2026 to shareowners of record on March 13, 2026.
Between September 27, 2025 and December 31, 2025, Coca-Cola repurchased 1,447,290 shares for US$99.8m. This brought total buybacks under the February 21, 2019 authorization to 83,051,256 shares for US$5,158.45m, representing 1.92% of the company.

NVIDIA (NasdaqGS:NVDA) is rolling out OpenAI's Codex AI coding agent, powered by GPT-5.5, across its global workforce.
More than 10,000 employees are already using the agent across engineering, product, sales, marketing, finance, and operations.
The deployment runs on NVIDIA's GB200 NVL72 computers with public backing from Jensen Huang and Sam Altman.
The initiative positions NVIDIA as a large scale internal adopter of AI agents, potentially serving as a reference case for enterprise customers.
NVIDIA, trading at around $208.27 per share, has delivered very large multi year returns, including about 87.7% over the past year and roughly 7x over three years. For investors tracking NasdaqGS:NVDA, this new Codex rollout adds another concrete example of how the company is using AI internally, not just supplying chips and systems to others.
The company wide use of an AI coding and productivity agent gives you a real world case study of how AI tools may reshape workflows inside a major tech supplier. As customers assess AI infrastructure and agent adoption, NVIDIA's own deployment could become part of the commercial story around its hardware and software stack.

Meta Platforms has removed certain law firm advertisements that were soliciting plaintiffs for addiction related lawsuits targeting its core social media platforms. The shift comes after multiple trial defeats and a rapid increase in legal actions that challenge the design and youth safety features of Meta's services. More than 3,000 lawsuits are active in California, and recent verdicts have introduced new obligations that could affect Meta's legal and compliance approach. For investors tracking NasdaqGS:META, this legal turn sits alongside a share price of $629.86 and very large 3 year gains of about 190.0%, as well as a 110.0% return over 5 years. The stock is also up 18.9% over the past year and 9.9% over the past week, while year to date performance shows a 3.2% decline and a 2.6% return over the past month. This illustrates that shorter term moves have been mixed even as multi year returns remain strong. The surge in litigation and Meta's response to plaintiff advertising add another layer of risk for a business already under scrutiny on youth safety, content, and platform design. Readers assessing NasdaqGS:META now have to weigh near term legal, reputational, and regulatory pressures alongside product and AI related headlines when thinking about the company's operating environment and potential volatility.

Microsoft (MSFT, Financials) is back in focus after a court filing showed Elon Musk sought settlement talks with OpenAI shortly before their federal trial began.

The filing said Musk contacted OpenAI President Greg Brockman two days before trial to discuss a possible deal. The case centers on Musk's claim that OpenAI abandoned its nonprofit mission by shifting toward a for-profit structure.
Musk is seeking leadership changes and $150 billion in damages from OpenAI and Microsoft, one of OpenAI's largest investors.
The trial began April 28 in Oakland and is expected to last several weeks. OpenAI executives and Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella are expected to testify later this month.
For investors, the case adds legal uncertainty around one of Microsoft's most important AI partnerships.

Lyft’s modeled fair value was trimmed from US$20.31 to US$19.28, signaling a small reset in the price targets underpinning many recent research notes. Analysts are tying this shift to cautious revisions in their assumptions and to mixed reactions around Lyft’s growing work with Nvidia on autonomous driving, where both execution risk and potential upside feature prominently. As you read on, you will see how this evolving analyst narrative might shape the way you track Lyft from here.

RBC Capital highlights Lyft’s partnership with Nvidia as a way to build a hybrid autonomous vehicle and human driver platform, which the firm views as a potential source of a stronger long term moat despite expected stock volatility.
The broadened AV ecosystem that includes Nvidia and multiple partners is framed by RBC Capital as a way for Lyft to share development costs and access turnkey AV capabilities rather than building everything in house.
A long list of firms, including JPMorgan, Truist, Mizuho, BofA, Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo, Evercore ISI, Cantor Fitzgerald and others, have all reduced their Lyft price targets in 2026, pointing to more cautious modeling on the stock.
RBC Capital flags competition from Waymo and other well funded AV players as a possible source of pressure on Lyft’s 2026 results, which feeds into valuation debates around how quickly AV initiatives can contribute.
Several firms that updated their models, such as Mizuho and Morgan Stanley, are still working with price targets in the mid teens, which signals tempered expectations on execution and growth relative to earlier assumptions.

An Israeli ministerial committee approves a bill that would allow ride hailing services like Lyft and Uber to operate in the country, opening the door to potential entry into a new market if the legislation progresses.
Lyft announces a broad collaboration with NVIDIA AI that covers enterprise AI infrastructure, next generation mapping, and future Level 4 autonomous fleet architectures built on NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion.
Lyft is added to several S&P indices, including the S&P 600, S&P 600 Industrials sector, S&P 1000, and S&P Composite 1500, bringing the stock into a wider set of index tracking portfolios.
The Board of Directors authorizes a new share repurchase program of up to US$1,000m of Class A common stock after completing a prior US$500.05m program that covered 30,281,000 shares, or 7.33% of the company.
Fair Value moved from US$20.31 to US$19.28, representing a small reduction in modeled equity value.
Revenue Growth shifted from 11.62% to 11.51% in the projections.
Net Profit Margin is modeled at 5.23%, unchanged from the prior 5.23% assumption.
The future P/E multiple was adjusted from 19.02x to 18.10x.
The Discount Rate moved from 8.45% to 8.48% in the model.

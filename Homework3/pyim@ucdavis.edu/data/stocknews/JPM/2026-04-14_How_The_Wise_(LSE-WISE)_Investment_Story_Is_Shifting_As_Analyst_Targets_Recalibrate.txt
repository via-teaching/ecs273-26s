Wise’s refreshed fair value price target edges from £11.62 to £11.60, signalling only a minor recalibration in where analysts see the shares settling based on current assumptions. That small move sits against a backdrop where some firms are trimming targets while others keep them higher, leaving you with a mixed but finely tuned view on how much upside remains. Read on to see how these shifting targets fit together and how you can track the evolving story around Wise.
Analyst Price Targets don't always capture the full story. Head over to our Company Report to find new ways to value Wise.
JPMorgan has kept an Overweight rating on Wise while adjusting its price target to £12.25 from £13.85. This signals continued confidence in the company’s execution even as assumptions are fine tuned.
The earlier decision by JPMorgan to lift its target by £0.10 in January points to interest in Wise’s longer term growth prospects. The valuation tweaks reflect updates to its model rather than a complete change in stance.
Cantor Fitzgerald’s upgrade in January indicates a more positive view on where Wise can go from here. It adds another supportive voice to the bull case alongside JPMorgan.
UBS has taken a more cautious line, cutting its Wise price target by £0.80. This suggests increased focus on risks around execution, competition or the assumptions used in valuing the shares.
The move by JPMorgan to bring its target down from £13.85 to £12.25 shows that even supporters are revisiting their upside scenarios and are more selective on what they are willing to pay for Wise today.

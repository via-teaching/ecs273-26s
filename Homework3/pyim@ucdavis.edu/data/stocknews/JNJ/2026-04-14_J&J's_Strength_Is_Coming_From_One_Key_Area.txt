Johnson & Johnson (NYSE:JNJ)just delivered a solid quarter and it's becoming pretty clearwhat's driving the story right now.
The company brought in $24.1 billion in revenue for Q1, beating expectations, while earnings came in at $2.70 per share. That's slightly lower than last year, but still ahead of what analysts were looking for. On top of that, J&J raised its full-year outlook, which usually tells you management is feeling confident about what's ahead.

The real strength is coming from its cancer drugs. Darzalex continues to be a major contributor, bringing in close to $4 billion for the quarter, while newer therapies like Carvykti are growing quickly. That helped push the Innovative Medicine segment up about 11% year over year.
At the same time, there are some clear offsets. Stelara, which lost exclusivity in the U.S., is declining sharply, and a few older products are starting to lose momentum.
The MedTech business is doing its part, growing steadily and adding some balance to the overall picture.

The McDonald's Corporation (MCD,Financials) wants to add newenergy drinks and specialty sodas to allof its U.S. outlets this year.The business is likely to add things like Dirty Dr Pepper, Red Bull Dragonberry Energizer, and Mango Pineapple Refresher. Next month, specialty sodas will be available, and energy drinks will start to become available in August.McDonald's wants to sell these drinks for less than its competitors, such as Starbucks Corporation and Dutch Bros Inc., to make them more accessible in a segment that is growing quickly.The change is due to more people wanting energy drinks and personalized sodas, which has led restaurant chains to add more items to their menus. Yum! Brands, Inc. has already added comparable drinks to Taco Bell, which shows that the industry is starting to use them more widely.For McDonald's, the expansion could help bring in more customers and greater average order values, especially from younger customers. Investors will be monitoring to see if new drinks can bring in more money and make the company more competitive in the quick-service market.

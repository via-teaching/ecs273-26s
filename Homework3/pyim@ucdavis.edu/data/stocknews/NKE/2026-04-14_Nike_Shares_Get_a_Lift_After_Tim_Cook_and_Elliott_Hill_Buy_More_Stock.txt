Apple Chief Executive Tim Cook and Nike CEO Elliott Hill just gave Nike’s stock another boost. The sneaker company's stock price rose 2% in after-hours trading after Hill and Cook, the lead director on Nike’s board, disclosed they had bought nearly 50,000 shares in Nike between them. Cook and Hill's purchases provided a jolt of support for Nike, whose turnaround has hit snags--especially in China.

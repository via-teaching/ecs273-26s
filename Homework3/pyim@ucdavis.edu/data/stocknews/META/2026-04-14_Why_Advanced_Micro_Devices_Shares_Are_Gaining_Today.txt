Pu pointed to AMD's server unit performance, citing industry data that showed the company held a 41 percent value share of the market in the fourth quarter of 2025. Shipment share stood at 29 percent for the period.
The analyst projected that AMD's full-year 2026 server shipments could rise 28 percent from the prior year while revenue may climb about 46 percent. He cited increased wafer capacity and higher average selling prices tied to upcoming Venice processors.

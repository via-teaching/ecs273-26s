Amazon (NASDAQ:AMZN) shares rose about 4% on Tuesday after the company said it plans to acquire Globalstar and expand its low Earth orbit satellite network to offer direct-to-device connectivity.
The acquisitionwouldintegrate Globalstar's satellite assets into Amazon's Leo network. Amazon also disclosed a separate agreement with Apple (NASDAQ:AAPL) to provide satellite services for iPhone and Apple Watch users. The arrangement may allow texting emergency services and roadside assistance when cellular coverage is unavailable.

Amazon expects the Globalstar transaction to close in 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and other conditions. The company said Globalstar shareholders may elect to receive cash or Amazon shares as consideration, with standard proration mechanics applied.
The satellite push positions Amazon to compete more directly with Starlink in the emerging market for space-based mobile connectivity. Separately, Amazon expanded its Amazon Autos platform this week beyond an initial partnership with Hyundai to include additional mainstream automotive brands.
Amazon shares have gained ground in recent sessions amid broader strength in technology names. The Globalstar news may add to investor focus on Amazon's infrastructure ambitions.

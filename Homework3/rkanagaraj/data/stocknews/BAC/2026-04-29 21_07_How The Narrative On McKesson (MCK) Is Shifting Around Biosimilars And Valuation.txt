McKesson's fair value estimate has shifted only slightly, moving from US$991.67 to US$990.87, which keeps the core valuation picture broadly intact. Around that small model change, analysts are adjusting individual price targets in different directions as they weigh the value of the biosimilar pipeline, co manufacturing optionality, and the current premium to the three year average. Read on to see how these price target moves fit into the evolving McKesson story and what to watch next in the analyst narrative.
Stay updated as the Fair Value for McKesson shifts by adding it to your watchlist or portfolio. Alternatively, explore our Community to discover new perspectives on McKesson.
BofA increased its McKesson target to US$1,040 in March, highlighting potential value in the biosimilar opportunity and co manufacturing optionality after the company received a new National Drug Code for a Neulasta biosimilar.
Barclays lifted its target to US$1,050 in February and kept an Overweight rating, citing McKesson as a potential relative safe haven within healthcare while still acknowledging the current premium to the three year average.
Several firms including JPMorgan, BofA, Mizuho, Wells Fargo, TD Cowen, and Morgan Stanley raised price targets in early February, signaling that many covering analysts see support for higher valuation levels based on execution to date.
William Blair recently initiated coverage with a bullish stance, adding another supportive voice to the existing group of positive brokers around the stock.
Evercore ISI cut its target in April and placed McKesson on its Tactical Underperform list, pointing readers to a more cautious stance on near term risk reward.
UBS and BofA both lowered price targets in April, suggesting some reassessment of upside as investors weigh biosimilar execution risks and the share price premium highlighted by Barclays.

McKesson announced that long serving Executive Vice President and CFO Britt Vitalone plans to retire after a 20 year career with the company, and will move into a strategic advisor role to support the planned separation of Medical Surgical Solutions and help with the transition.
Kenny Cheung is set to join McKesson as CFO effective May 29, 2026, bringing experience from Sysco where he oversaw global financial planning and analysis, accounting, audit, tax, and corporate finance.
Between October 1, 2025 and December 31, 2025, McKesson repurchased 800,000 shares for US$652.29m, representing 0.65% of shares under its buyback program announced on May 24, 2018.
Since the start of that buyback program, McKesson has repurchased 64,000,000 shares, or 41.75% of shares, for a total of US$18.43b.
The fair value estimate has changed from US$991.67 to US$990.87, a reduction of about 0.1%.

The future P/E assumption has changed from 23.05x to 23.04x.

Baker Hughes is back in focus as the average fair value price target shifts from US$62.67 to US$69.33, a move of about US$6.66 per share. Recent research shows multiple global and regional firms, including JPMorgan, revisiting their models within a short window and lifting targets in increments that range from roughly US$3 to US$14. As you read on, you will see how this cluster of price target resets is shaping the evolving narrative around Baker Hughes and what to watch next.
Analyst Price Targets don't always capture the full story. Head over to our Company Report to find new ways to value Baker Hughes.
A wide set of firms, including JPMorgan, Citi, TD Cowen, Stifel, Jefferies, BMO Capital, BofA and Evercore ISI, have lifted their Baker Hughes price targets in recent months, with individual changes ranging roughly from US$3 to US$14, which signals increased conviction in their valuation frameworks.
Several of the larger upward revisions, such as the US$14 moves from JPMorgan and Evercore ISI and double digit moves from Citi, TD Cowen, Stifel, BMO Capital and BofA, point to analyst models that now support a higher fair value range for the shares.
Revisions clustered in April 2026 from houses like Susquehanna, UBS, Barclays, RBC Capital and Piper Sandler add to the breadth of coverage, giving you a dense set of updated views around Baker Hughes execution and growth prospects.
Even with many higher targets, research commentary can still flag risks around execution, end market demand and capital allocation, which may limit how much confidence investors place in any single price objective.
The spread in target changes, from around US$3 at firms like RBC Capital and Piper Sandler to much larger moves at JPMorgan or Jefferies, suggests analysts do not share a single view on Baker Hughes potential, so investors may want to treat the new average target as one data point rather than a clear signal.

Baker Hughes is reportedly reviewing a potential sale of its Waygate Technologies industrial testing and inspection unit, with the asset said to be valued at around US$1.5b and a formal sales process possibly starting in the coming months.
The company agreed to supply three NovaLT™16 gas turbines with centrifugal compressors and services for a natural gas pipeline project in Argentina, supporting gas transport from the Vaca Muerta formation to two floating LNG vessels.
Baker Hughes and Google Cloud are collaborating on AI driven power optimization and sustainability solutions for data centers, combining turbomachinery and power systems expertise with cloud based AI and data analytics.
Baker Hughes received an award from Twenty20 Energy to deliver 10 Frame 5 gas turbines and generator technology for up to 250 MW of power capacity for data center projects in Georgia and Texas, with initial deliveries scheduled for 2027.

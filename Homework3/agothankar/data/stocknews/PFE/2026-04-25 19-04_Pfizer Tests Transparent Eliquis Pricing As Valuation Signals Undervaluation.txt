Pfizer (NYSE:PFE) and Bristol Myers Squibb are making their blood thinner Eliquis available through the Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drug Company platform. The move brings a blockbuster prescription medicine into a lower cost, direct to consumer channel aimed at greater price transparency. This collaboration focuses on expanding patient access to Eliquis while testing alternative distribution and pricing approaches for high demand drugs. For investors following NYSE:PFE, this development sits at the intersection of branded pharmaceuticals, pricing reform, and changing distribution models. Pfizer is engaging directly with a cash pay, transparent pricing platform that has attracted attention from patients looking for predictable out of pocket costs. Readers may want to watch how volumes, patient mix, and relationships with traditional pharmacy benefit managers respond to this type of arrangement. The Eliquis move could serve as a reference point if Pfizer chooses to place additional products on similar platforms or expand this collaboration over time.

Several firms, including UBS, HSBC, Citi, Barclays, JPMorgan, BofA, Morgan Stanley, RBC Capital and Stifel, have raised Johnson & Johnson price targets, pointing to updated views on key products such as Tremfya, Tecvayli, Inlexzo and Darzalex, as well as late stage pipeline assets. UBS and HSBC both moved targets to US$280, while Citi, Barclays and JPMorgan also lifted their levels. This indicates that a number of large houses see room for the shares to support higher valuation ranges than before. BofA, Citi and JPMorgan commentary links target changes to adjustments in revenue expectations or product assumptions. This gives you specific levers to watch, such as uptake and treatment duration in immunology and oncology franchises. Not all firms shifted to an outright positive stance, with JPMorgan and BofA maintaining Neutral ratings even as they raised price targets. This signals ongoing questions around execution, competitive intensity and how fully current expectations already reflect the pipeline. The spread in targets, from around US$250 at JPMorgan and BofA to US$280 at UBS and HSBC, highlights that analysts are not aligned on the risk reward trade off. The court has not yet ruled on the merits. A Philadelphia jury awarded US$250,000 in compensatory and punitive damages in an ovarian cancer case tied to talc products.

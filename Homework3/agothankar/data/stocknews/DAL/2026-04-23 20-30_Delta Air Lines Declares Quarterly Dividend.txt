Title: Delta Air Lines Declares Quarterly Dividend
Date: 2026-04-23 20:30
URL: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/delta-air-lines-declares-quarterly-203000974.html?.tsrc=rss

ATLANTA,April 23, 2026/PRNewswire/ -- Delta Air Lines' (NYSE:DAL) Board of Directors today declared a quarterly dividend of $0.1875 per share. The dividend is payable to shareholders of record as of the close of business on May 14, 2026, and will be paid on June 4, 2026.

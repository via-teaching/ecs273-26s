Title: Amazon Stock Rallies To Record High On Meta AI Chip Deal
Date: 2026-04-24 20:31
URL: https://finance.yahoo.com/m/2bcc6056-f7fb-3067-b49b-3d8a0ef63308/amazon-stock-rallies-to.html?.tsrc=rss

Amazon stock jumped Friday to a record high following news it will provide its Graviton processors to Facebook parent Meta Platforms. Meta stock gained as well. Amazon said Meta will use "tens of millions" of Amazon Web Services' Graviton CPUs (central processing units) to power its AI offerings, according to the announcement.

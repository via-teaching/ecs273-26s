Bank of America (NYSE:BAC) is helping lead a US$16b financing package for a new data center campus in Michigan.
The project is designed to support AI infrastructure needs for Oracle and OpenAI.
The financing positions Bank of America as a key structuring agent and financial advisor on one of the largest purpose built data center projects in the US.
For Bank of America, this deal sits at the intersection of traditional corporate banking and the build out of AI focused digital infrastructure. As hyperscalers, software providers, and AI players look for large scale, power hungry facilities, banks with the balance sheet depth and structuring experience to support multi billion dollar projects can become important partners.
Readers watching NYSE:BAC may view this kind of financing activity as one indication of where the bank is allocating expertise and capital. Exposure to AI related infrastructure, alongside more established lending and advisory businesses, could influence how the market views the bank’s role in large technology and data projects.

Title:   Standard Chartered Takes Stake In Crypto Trading Firm GSR
Date:    2026-05-05 12-38
URL:     https://www.cryptoprowl.com/releases/standard-chartered-takes-stake-in-crypto-trading-firm-gsr-4378?.tsrc=rss
Content: British bank Standard Chartered (LON: $STAN ) has taken a $150 million U.S. stake in cryptocurrency trading firm GSR. The investment values GSR at $1 billion U.S. GSR is a crypto and capital markets trading firm established by former Goldman Sachs (NYSE: $GS ) bankers.
 
Standard Chartered made the investment through its venture capital division called “SC Ventures.” The bank is seeking to expand its digital asset services. In a news release, GSR said the deal will help it to further bridge traditional finance and digital assets, and to expand access to tokenized securities such as stocks and bonds. Standard Chartered has made several recent investments aimed at expanding its crypto offerings. In 2025, Standard Chartered launched crypto trading for institutional clients, becoming one of the first global banks to offer spot Bitcoin (CRYPTO: $BTC ) trading.  STAN stock has risen 73% over the last 12 months to trade at $1,821.80 British pounds per share in London.

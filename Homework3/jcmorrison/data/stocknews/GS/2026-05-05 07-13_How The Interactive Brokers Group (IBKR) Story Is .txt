Goldman Sachs has taken one of the strongest positive stances, first lifting its price target in early April and then again on 22 April, before adding Interactive Brokers to its US Conviction List on 1 May. This signals high conviction in the stock within its broader coverage. BMO Capital, Piper Sandler and Barclays each raised their price targets on 22 April, suggesting that several research desks see room for the current valuation to absorb updated assumptions around the business and its execution. The cluster of target increases in April points to supportive Street sentiment around the company’s ability to grow its franchise, with multiple banks refining models rather than stepping away from the story. Jefferies moved in the opposite direction on 6 April, cutting its price target. This highlights that not all analysts are aligned on how much upside is embedded in the stock at present levels. Keefe Bruyette resumed coverage on 8 April with a Market Perform rating, signalling a more balanced view where execution and growth prospects are seen as more fairly reflected in the current share price. Interactive Brokers declared a quarterly dividend of US$0.0875 per share, scheduled for payment on 12 June 2026, with an ex dividend and record date of 1 June 2026. The company launched crypto asset trading for eligible individual investors in the EEA through Interactive Brokers Ireland Limited. It offers 24/7 access to 11 crypto assets on the same platform as traditional securities, with stated commissions of 0.12% to 0.18% and no custody fees. IBKR clients can now transfer supported cryptocurrencies from external wallets into linked crypto accounts and trade them alongside stocks, options, futures, currencies, bonds and other assets, with commissions of 0.12% to 0.18% and a US$1.75 minimum per order. Interactive Brokers Group, Inc. was added to the FTSE All World Index (USD) and introduced new offerings for Swedish and Canadian investors, including ISK accounts with access to global derivatives and portfolio lending, and a Registered Retirement Income Fund solution in Canada.

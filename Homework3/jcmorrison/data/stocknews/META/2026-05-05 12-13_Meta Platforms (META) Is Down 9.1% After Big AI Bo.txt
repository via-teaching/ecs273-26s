To own Meta today, you have to be comfortable with a company pouring very large sums into AI infrastructure while its core ad business remains the profit engine. The latest quarter reinforced that core strength but also highlighted the key short term catalyst and risk: whether sharply higher, AI focused capex and related debt issues translate into durable monetization before regulatory, legal and governance pressures around content and youth safety start to bite.
